Why Cartback?

A shopper puts the product in the cart and then drops off at checkout. Most stores never notice. Cartback sends the person who left a short reminder. You pay only for the texts that go out.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When will Cartback launch?

It's in development. Leave your email and we'll tell you the day it's out.

Which store platforms will it work with?

First targets are Shopify and WooCommerce. Other platforms will be assessed before launch.

Is it allowed to text shoppers?

Marketing texts need the shopper's consent, and rules differ by country. Cartback is designed not to text numbers without consent; the store owner's responsibilities will be spelled out before launch.

Can I cancel?

There is no subscription. When you buy a pack, the texts left show in the dashboard, and you can stop any time.
