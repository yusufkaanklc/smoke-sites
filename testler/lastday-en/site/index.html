Why Lastday?

Most return windows start the day the package arrives, and nobody puts them in a calendar. Lastday counts down every order's last day and keeps the warranty and receipt next to it. Getting your money back starts with knowing the date.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When will Lastday launch?

It's in development. Leave your email and we'll tell you the day it's out.

How does it know the return window?

You add the order; the window follows the store's policy or the date you enter. The goal is to suggest the common 30-day window automatically.

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. Lastday is being designed to work without a bank connection.

Can I cancel anytime?

Subscriptions will be managed through the App Store and Google Play, so you'll be able to cancel whenever you like.
